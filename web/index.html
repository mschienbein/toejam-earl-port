First and second standard-mapped gamepads control players 1 and 2. Connect player 2 above for its keyboard, or attach a second gamepad. Press a gamepad button after connecting it. Input is released and the game pauses when the tab is hidden. Click Play to resume.

Use an up-to-date browser on HTTPS (or localhost). WebGPU is preferred; WebGL 2 is the automatic fallback. Web Audio starts on a click. Audio sample blocks are transferred to an AudioWorklet without SharedArrayBuffer or cross-origin-isolation headers.

Volume, scaling and the player-2 keyboard connection are saved locally. This build runs the original cartridge through the hybrid host with an empty translation registry; it does not claim to be an all-native game-logic implementation.
